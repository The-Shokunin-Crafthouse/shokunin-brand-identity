Color · the room and the signal

Color earns its place by doing work.

Two warm grays carry the room. One vermilion does the signaling. Status pulls its hue from outside the brand band, so brand and danger can never be confused. Every value below was placed to solve a specific problem.

iThe room · warm-gray ladder

Thirteen steps. One hue.

The page sits at warmGray.100. Ink sits at warmGray.800. The other eleven steps exist so the surface between them never has to fight to read.

iiiOutside the band · status

Four hues. None of them shu.

Error is oxblood, not red. When a screen says danger, brand and warning can't be mistaken. Each status carries a light pair and a dark pair, both calibrated AA.

ivResolution · three tiers

Primitive · semantic · component.

Components consume only the middle column. Raw hex at the call site is a bug. The resolution path below is the only one the system permits.

Why the prefix--sc-p-* is primitive. --sc-* is semantic. The hyphen-p is the only signal you're reaching past the system.
