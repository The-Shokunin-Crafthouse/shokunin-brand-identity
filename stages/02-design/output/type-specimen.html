The scale, end to end

Read top to bottom: chrome at the top, body in the middle, display at the foot. Weight follows the optical band — medium for chrome, regular for reading, medium again at mid-headline, light for display. The rationale per step lives in _config/design-system/type-system.md.

04 · OPTICAL · NHG AT 11 / 12

Why medium, not regular, in chrome

At 11 and 12 pixels, NHG regular loses stem density under sub-pixel rendering — the strokes alias to neighbouring rows and the line of caps reads visibly uneven. Medium restores presence without breaking the weight rhythm. The two rows below show the difference: top row uses regular (the wrong call), bottom row uses medium (locked).
